Perspective

Most hospitality problems aren’t concept problems

The strongest restaurants aren’t defined by their menus or their design. They’re defined by how they’re led, how the team is held, and how consistently the operation performs.

That’s where things tend to break down, and where LPP focuses.

Services

How LPP works with you

Operational
Review

A structured look at how your operation is actually running. This includes service flow, labor model, management structure and daily execution. It identifies what’s working, what isn’t, and what to address first.

Opening
Support

Hands-on guidance from pre-opening through launch. This includes staffing, training, service standards and operational readiness so the team is prepared before the first guest walks in.

Leadership
Development

Direct work with your management team to sharpen role clarity, build stronger routines and raise accountability. For teams that want better managers, not just more of them.

Owner
Advisory

An independent operator’s perspective for investors, developers and ownership groups navigating growth, transitions or structural decisions. Practical, direct and grounded in how hospitality businesses actually work.

Method

The work doesn’t stop
between engagements.

Every LPP engagement is supported by continuous operational analysis across financials, guest sentiment and menu performance. Between conversations, a proprietary reporting environment tracks what matters and surfaces what warrants attention. Recommendations stay current. Nothing important waits for the next meeting.

Continuous financial review

P&L analysis across revenue, COGS, payroll and margin, interpreted against your specific operation rather than a generic benchmark.

Guest sentiment analysis

Review synthesis across platforms, surfaced as operational themes. What guests are consistently saying, and what it points to.

Menu performance

Engineering signals across your menu: what is performing, what is not, and where the opportunity lies between margin and guest preference.

Emerging operational risks

Alerts triggered automatically when a metric crosses a threshold that warrants attention, before issues compound.
